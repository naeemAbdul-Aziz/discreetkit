# Refund & Return Policy

**DiscreetKit - Privacy Infrastructure for Health**  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Last Updated:** April 2026

## 1. No-Refund Policy
Due to the sensitive, clinical, and hygienic nature of the products sold via DiscreetKit (including diagnostic kits, contraceptives, and personal health items), **we maintain a strict No-Refund and No-Return policy.**

Once a product has been delivered and the packaging seal is broken, it cannot be returned for a refund, credit, or exchange. This is a critical safety measure to prevent the re-circulation of potentially tampered medical items and to ensure the health of our community.

## 2. Exceptions
We only offer refunds or replacements under the following specific circumstances:
*   **Damaged Goods:** If the product arrives physically damaged or the primary seal is broken *at the point of delivery*.
*   **Incorrect Item:** If the item delivered does not match the item ordered.
*   **Expired Products:** If the product supplied has passed its official expiration date at the time of delivery.

## 3. Claim Process
To initiate a claim for an exception:
1.  **Immediate Notification:** The customer must notify DiscreetKit support within **one (1) hour** of receiving the order.
2.  **Photographic Proof:** Clear photos of the damaged/incorrect item and its packaging must be provided.
3.  **Verification:** Our team will verify the claim with the fulfilling Pharmacy Partner.

## 4. Refund Method
If an exception is granted, refunds will be processed via the original payment method (Paystack) within 5-7 business days. Alternatively, a replacement item may be dispatched at no additional cost.

## 5. Cancellations
Orders can only be cancelled if the rider has not yet been dispatched to the pharmacy. Once a rider is en route for pickup, the order is final and non-refundable.
