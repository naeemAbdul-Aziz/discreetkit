# Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)

**DiscreetKit - Privacy Infrastructure for Health**  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Last Updated:** April 2026

## 1. Introduction
This Acceptable Use Policy ("Policy") outlines the rules and guidelines for using the DiscreetKit platform. Our mission is to provide safe, anonymous access to health and wellness products. To maintain the integrity of our network, all partners and users must adhere to this policy.

## 2. Approved Products
DiscreetKit is strictly for the delivery of health, wellness, and diagnostic products that meet the following criteria:
*   Approved by the Food and Drugs Authority (FDA) of Ghana.
*   Licensed for sale by the Pharmacy Council of Ghana.
*   Non-prescription diagnostic kits (e.g., HIV, Malaria, Pregnancy).
*   General health and wellness products (e.g., contraceptives, sanitary products).

## 3. Prohibited Items and Activities
The following items and activities are strictly prohibited on the DiscreetKit platform:
*   **Illegal Substances:** Narcotics, unapproved drugs, or controlled substances.
*   **Prescription-Only Medication (POM):** Sale of POMs without valid clinical verification and regulatory oversight is prohibited.
*   **Off-Platform Transactions:** Attempting to bypass the platform for payment or fulfillment to avoid compliance monitoring.
*   **Data Misuse:** Storing or sharing customer data beyond what is required for immediate order fulfillment.

## 4. Partner/Vendor Compliance
All Pharmacy Partners must:
*   Maintain a valid and current Pharmacy Council License.
*   Ensure all products are genuine and within their expiration dates.
*   Adhere to the "Discreet Packing" protocol (unbranded, opaque packaging).
*   Report any suspected fraudulent activity immediately to DiscreetKit Admin.

## 5. Violations and Enforcement
DiscreetKit reserves the right to suspend or terminate any partner or user account that violates this Policy. We cooperate with law enforcement and regulatory bodies (Pharmacy Council, FDA) to report illegal activities.
