# Legal & Compliance Documentation

This directory contains official policy documents for DiscreetKit. These are used for regulatory compliance (Pharmacy Council, FDA) and payment partner onboarding (Paystack).

## Policies
*   [Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)](./ACCEPTABLE_USE_POLICY.md) - Guidelines for platform usage and product listing.
*   [Refund & Return Policy](./REFUND_POLICY.md) - Terms regarding order cancellations and product returns.

## Internal Notes
*   These documents should be referenced in all partner agreements.
*   Any changes to the Refund Policy must be approved by the Legal/Operations lead due to the impact on pharmacy settlements.
