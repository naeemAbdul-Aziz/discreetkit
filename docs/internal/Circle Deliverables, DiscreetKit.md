# Circle Deliverables, DiscreetKit

## Overview
- Purpose: Unified, exhaustive deliverables and checks for every Circle and the Operations Hub, written in simple, non-technical language.
- Scope: Public site, Waitlist Portal, copy, visuals, flows, privacy signals, compliance language, trust markers, and readiness for pharmacy onboarding.
- Principles: Sweat the small stuff; verify reality; keep language respectful, consistent, and aligned to the "Ghost" narrative.

## Roles at a Glance
- Partnerships Circle: Validates supply-side accuracy and credibility across site; ensures reality-based claims and LOI proofing.
- Marketing Circle: Owns tone, narrative, and clarity; confirms all entry points lead to the right action.
- Product & Technical Circle: Ensures flows are smooth, private, and reliable; fixes UX frictions and edge cases.
- Legal & Compliance Circle: Ensures language and data practices meet policy and regulatory standards; flags risk.
- Medical & Research Circle: Verifies health accuracy, SKU correctness, and safe language; removes medical ambiguities.
- Operations Hub: Executes, schedules content, coordinates riders and field work, runs QA cadence, and reports outcomes.

## Partnerships Circle (Supply & Alliances)
Mandate: Secure the physical network.

### Deliverables
- Supply reality memo: partners, locations, signed LOIs, pending; time-stamped.
- Corrections log: where site claims were adjusted for accuracy.
- Updated partner copy and FAQ snippets.

### Website Checks (Non-Technical)
- Names & Locations:
  - Pharmacy names match real partners across pages and forms.
  - Neighborhoods (Legon/Madina/UPSA) spelled correctly and used consistently.
  - "Coming soon" areas clearly labeled; no implied coverage.
- Credibility Signals:
  - LOI count accurate with date (e.g., “10 signed as of [date]”).
  - Partner logos permitted; no placeholders.
  - Brief note on vetting process (one sentence).
- Delivery & Logistics:
  - Delivery windows realistic and clear (same-day, next-day).
  - Rider safety and discretion language present but modest.
- Contact & Outreach:
  - "Partner with us" CTA visible; form works and is concise.

### Micro-Checks
- Pharmacy name spelling and capitalization.
- No stock photos implying false coverage.
- Polite form success messages with next steps.

## Marketing Circle (Demand & Growth)
Mandate: Control the narrative and drive user acquisition.

### Deliverables
- Brand tone sheet: examples of right vs. wrong phrasing.
- Updated copy for home, waitlist, FAQs.
- A/B test plan: two headline/CTA variants.

### Website Checks (Non-Technical)
- Tone & Language:
  - Plain English; consistent CTAs (“Join the Waitlist”).
  - Microcopy respects privacy and dignity.
- Story & Flow:
  - One-sentence explanation of DiscreetKit above the fold.
  - Effortless path: homepage → waitlist → confirmation.
- Visuals & Trust:
  - Neutral, discreet imagery; readable brand colors.
  - Trust badges present where helpful, never overbearing.
- Metrics & Proof:
  - Counters update reliably or state "approximate"; avoid undated growth claims.

### Micro-Checks
- CTA consistency and grammar across pages.
- Mobile spacing and line breaks; avoid orphans.
- Avoid slang/emojis that dilute discretion.

## Product & Technical Circle (Infrastructure)
Mandate: Build and secure the platform.

### Deliverables
- Flow audit report with quick fixes.
- Privacy microcopy for data-handling moments.
- Edge-case weekly QA checklist.

### Website Checks (Non-Technical)
- Flows & Edge Cases:
  - Test waitlist with good, bad, and empty inputs.
  - Friendly, helpful error messages; clear loading states.
- Privacy & Safety:
  - Temporary data cleared as promised ("Auto-Wipe").
  - Undo/retry paths that feel safe.
  - Short privacy statements near sensitive actions.
- Accessibility & Mobile:
  - Readable text on small screens; sufficient color contrast.
  - Basic keyboard navigation; visible focus rings.
- Reliability Signals:
  - Avoid abrupt auto-scrolls.
  - Confirmation pages simple with next steps.

### Micro-Checks
- Use labels over placeholders in forms.
- Button states (disabled/hover/active) readable.
- Link destinations valid; back buttons don’t break state.

## Legal & Compliance Circle (Protection)
Mandate: Mitigate regulatory and operational risk.

### Deliverables
- Plain-English compliance glossary.
- Redline list of risky phrases removed or softened.
- Footer and form copy updates (consent, privacy clarity).

### Website Checks (Non-Technical)
- Policies & Notices:
  - Privacy and Terms current; match actual practices.
  - Clear consent wording at data collection points.
  - Simple cookie/tracking notice if used.
- Risk Language:
  - No guarantees implying liability (medical or delivery).
  - Disclaimers where advice is general, not personal.
- User Rights:
  - Easy path to request deletion or support.
  - Plain contact route for concerns.

### Micro-Checks
- Policy dates and “last updated” markers.
- Footer links to policies on all relevant pages.
- Consistent “Guardian” protocol naming.

## Medical & Research Circle (Trust & Safety)
Mandate: Validate the health standard.

### Deliverables
- Medical copy review memo: changes and rationale.
- Approved SKU list with short, neutral descriptions.
- FAQ corrections for clarity and neutrality.

### Website Checks (Non-Technical)
- SKU & Copy:
  - Product names/descriptions factual and neutral.
  - Brief safety notes where appropriate (general cautions).
  - Avoid personal medical advice.
- Accuracy & Clarity:
  - Replace ambiguous terms with simple explanations.
  - Non-judgmental language throughout FAQs.
- Trust Markers:
  - Short note on sourcing or quality checks.
  - Avoid trivializing or sensational graphics.

### Micro-Checks
- Dosage/time-window phrasing kept general and cautious.
- No slang/euphemisms in medical contexts.
- Consistent capitalization of product names.

## Operations Hub (Execution Layer)
Mandate: Day-to-day delivery and task completion.

### Cadence & Responsibilities
- Content Scheduling: Publish corrected copy with version notes; frequent small updates.
- Field Activations: Align site messaging with outreach; feed insights back to copy.
- Rider Coordination: Reflect delivery readiness accurately; update windows as needed.
- Customer Support: Keep a single, friendly voice; log common questions to improve FAQs.

### QA Rhythm
- Weekly site sweep using this document’s checklists.
- Pre-release mobile spot checks.
- Post-release confirmation of forms, CTAs, and policy links.

### Reporting
- Simple change log: what changed, why, and where.
- Weekly summary: wins, misses, next fixes.

## Unified “Sweat the Small Stuff” Checklist
- Grammar & Tone: Headlines, CTAs, microcopy match brand voice and "Ghost" narrative.
- Links: No 404s; back buttons behave.
- CTAs: Consistent wording; clear outcomes after click.
- Forms: Labels; friendly errors; confirmation with next steps.
- Privacy: Short statements near sensitive actions; no jargon.
- Visuals: Calm motion; readable spacing; sufficient contrast.
- Accessibility: Keyboard focus visible; alt text present.
- Trust Badges: Helpful, not overclaiming; dates on counters.
- Policy Access: Footer links visible; dates current.
- Mobile First: Test on small screens; tap targets comfortable.
- Location Mentions: Legon/Madina/UPSA spelling and reality alignment.
- Manifest/Robots/Sitemap: Present and clean; sensible titles/descriptions.
- Error/Loading: Polite messages; clear retry guidance.

## Beta Tester Engagement (Pre-Pharmacy Onboarding)
Goal: Respect testers’ time, gather useful signals, and show impact.

### Weekly Missions (15–20 minutes)
- Mission A: Join the waitlist; rate clarity 1–5; note confusion.
- Mission B: Read homepage; list two most trustworthy phrases.
- Mission C: Try a wrong form input; report error tone.
- Mission D: Find any broken links; submit one screenshot.

### Rewards & Communication
- Points system for missions; monthly small thank-you.
- Early-access badges for consistent contributors.
- Weekly kickoff message: what’s being tested and why.
- End-of-week appreciation + improvements shipped.

### Feedback Channels
- One lightweight form per mission; optional short voice note.
- 30-min office hours per week for live clarifications.

## Success Criteria
- No misleading claims; coverage references match reality.
- Clear, respectful, concise language everywhere.
- Waitlist and support flows feel effortless and private.
- Weekly small improvements instead of risky large drops.
- Beta testers feel valued; visible changes based on feedback.
