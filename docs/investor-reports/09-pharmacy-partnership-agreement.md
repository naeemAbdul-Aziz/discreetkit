## BACKGROUND
The Platform operates a specialized privacy-first logistics network designed to bridge the gap between healthcare providers and the youth demographic. We enable anonymous digital access to essential health products. The Partner possesses the inventory and license to dispense these products. The parties agree to collaborate to eliminate social barriers to healthcare.

---

## 1. PURPOSE
The Partner acts as an authorized fulfillment point (Node). The goal is to capture the "invisible" market (customers avoiding pharmacies due to stigma) by routing digital orders to the Partner for anonymous fulfillment.

## 2. RESPONSIBILITIES

### A. The Platform
*   **Demand Generation:** Handles all marketing and customer acquisition.
*   **Order Processing:** Digital intake, triage, and payment collection.
*   **Logistics:** Last-mile delivery via unbranded riders.
*   **Customer Support:** Handling all end-user inquiries and issues.

### B. The Partner
*   **Inventory:** Ensures consistent stock of Core SKUs (defined in Schedule A).
*   **Fulfillment:** Picks and packs orders using the **Blind Handoff** protocol.
*   **Compliance:** Ensures all dispensed products are valid (not expired) and compliant with the *Pharmacy Act, 1994*.
*   **SLA:** Adheres to the 10-minute response time for order acceptance.

---

## 3. COMMERCIAL TERMS

### A. Trade Discount Model
The Partner agrees to supply products to The Platform at a **Trade Discount of [20]%** off the standard retail shelf price.
*   *Logic:* This margin covers The Platform's operational costs (Payment fees, Marketing, Support).
*   *Example:* Item Retail Price = GHS 100. Platform remits GHS 80 to Partner. Platform Margin = GHS 20.

### B. Settlement Cycle (T+7)
*   **Reconciliation:** The Platform reconciles all fulfilled orders weekly (Monday - Sunday).
*   **Payout:** Total due is remitted to the Partner on the following **Tuesday**.
*   **Method:** Bank Transfer or Verified Mobile Money Merchant Wallet.

### C. Dynamic Pricing
*   **Schedule A:** Products and their agreed Trade Prices are defined in the Dashboard.
*   **Price Changes:** Must be updated in the Dashboard 48 hours in advance to allow platform updates.

---

## 4. OPERATIONAL STANDARDS

### A. Speed & Availability
*   **Acknowledgement:** Partner must accept "New Orders" on the Dashboard within **10 minutes**.
*   **Prep Time:** Package must be ready for rider pickup within **15 minutes** of acceptance.

### B. The "Blind Handoff" Protocol
*   **Packaging:** Items must be dispensed into opaque, sealed packaging (provided by The Platform or generic opaque bags).
*   **Privacy:** The rider must **NEVER** view the contents. The label should only show the `Order Code` (e.g., #DK-9921).

### C. Minimum Stock Levels
The Partner must maintain a safety buffer of **5 units** for Core SKUs:
1.  HIV Self-Test Kits
2.  Emergency Contraceptives (Plan B/Postinor)
3.  Pregnancy Test Kits

## 5. DATA PRIVACY & CONFIDENTIALITY

*   **Strict Anonymity:** The End-User's identity is strictly confidential. The Partner shall not record personal details beyond what is required for dispensing logs.
*   **No Direct Marketing:** The Partner agrees not to contact, market to, or solicit the Platform's customers directly.
*   **Data Protection:** Both parties warrant compliance with the **Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843)**.

---

## 6. INDEMNITY & LIABILITY

*   **Dispensing Errors:** The Partner indemnifies The Platform against claims arising from professional negligence (e.g., dispensing wrong/expired medication).
*   **Platform Liability:** The Platform is responsible for delivery/logistics issues *after* the fast handoff.
