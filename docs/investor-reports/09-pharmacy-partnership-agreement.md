**BACKGROUND**

The Platform operates a specialized privacy-first logistics network designed to bridge the gap between healthcare providers and the youth demographic. We enable anonymous digital access to essential health products under the ethos: **'Skip the Awkward'**. The Partner possesses the inventory and license to dispense these products. The parties agree to collaborate to eliminate social barriers to healthcare.

---

**1. PURPOSE**

The Partner acts as an authorized fulfillment point. The goal is to capture the 'invisible' market (customers avoiding pharmacies due to stigma) by routing digital orders to the Partner for anonymous fulfillment.

---

**2. RESPONSIBILITIES**

**A. The Platform:** Handles marketing, demand generation, digital order processing, payment collection, customer support, and **logistics orchestration** (via the Rider Registry protocol).

**B. The Partner:** Ensures stock of Core SKUs, picks and packs orders using the **Blind Handoff** protocol, and **manages dispatch** via assigned riders (either internal staff or platform-provided couriers) in accordance with the Rider Registry standards.

---

**3. COMMERCIAL TERMS**

• **Trade Discount:** The Partner agrees to supply products to The Platform at a **Trade Discount of [Trade Discount]** off the standard retail shelf price. This margin covers Platform operations. Definition: Item Retail Price = GHS 100. Platform remits GHS 90 (at 10%) to Partner.

• **Dynamic Pricing:** Specific product costs are defined in 'Schedule A'. Prices may be updated dynamically upon mutual written confirmation (Email or WhatsApp).

• **Settlement (T+7):** The Platform reconciles orders weekly (Monday - Sunday) and remits the total due to the Partner on the following **Tuesday** via Bank Transfer or Mobile Money.

---

**4. OPERATIONAL STANDARDS**

• **Response Time:** Upon receiving an order, the Partner must confirm availability within **10 minutes** and have the package ready within **15 minutes**.

• **Blind Handoff:** Items must be dispensed into opaque, sealed packaging (provided by the Platform). The rider must **never** view the contents.

• **Minimum Stock:** The Partner must maintain a buffer of **5 units** for Core SKUs:
1. HIV Self-Test Kits
2. Emergency Contraceptives (Plan B/Postinor)
3. Pregnancy Test Kits.

---

**5. DATA PRIVACY & AUDIT RIGHTS**

• **Confidentiality:** End-User identity is strictly confidential. The Partner must not contact, market to, or record personal details beyond dispensing requirements.

• **Data Silo:** The Partner agrees that their access to User Data is temporary and strictly limited to the time required to fulfill an active order. The Partner is prohibited from retaining such data.

• **Audit Right:** The Platform reserves the right to audit the Partner’s dispensing logs and inventory for items related to this Agreement at any time without prior notice to ensure compliance.

---

**6. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY & PROPRIETARY RIGHTS**

**6.1. Platform Ownership:** The Partner acknowledges that Access DiscreetKit Ltd retains all right, title, and interest in and to the 'Platform IP,' including the Codebase (Next.js/React), Algorithms (Smart Order Routing), Health Graph data, and Brand Assets ('DiscreetKit', 'Skip the Awkward').

**6.2. User Data Ownership:** Any data generated (User phone numbers, order contents, locations) is the **sole and exclusive property** of the Platform. The Partner acquires **no rights** to this data and is prohibited from copying, exporting, or retaining it for marketing/CRM.

**6.3. Limited License:** The Platform grants the Partner a limited, revocable license to display the 'Verified DiscreetKit Partner' badge solely for identification. The Partner may NOT use the brand to create competing digital products or 'white-label' services.

**6.4. Reverse Engineering:** The Partner shall not attempt to reverse engineer the Commerce Bot, mimic the 'Anonymous Ordering' flow, or access the API for unauthorized purposes.

**6.5. Feedback as Assignment:** Any suggestions provided by the Partner regarding the Platform’s operations automatically become the property of Access DiscreetKit Ltd without obligation of payment.

---

**7. RESTRICTIVE COVENANTS**

**7.1. Non-Circumvention & Non-Solicitation:** The Partner agrees NOT to solicit, market to, or divert The Platform’s customers. Any attempt to contact a customer outside the fulfillment purpose or insert marketing materials for competing services carries a mandatory penalty of GHS 50,000 per occurrence.

**7.2. Anti-Disparagement:** The Partner agrees never to make negative public statements, written or verbal, about the Platform, its founders, or its business model. This obligation survives termination indefinitely.

---

**8. LIABILITY & INDEMNITY**

**8.1. No Medical Practice:** The Platform is purely a logistics aggregator and does **not** practice medicine. The Partner retains **100% of the clinical liability** for the quality, efficacy, and dispensing of the products.

**8.2. Indemnity:** The Partner indemnifies the Platform against **ALL** claims arising from dispensing errors, expired stock, professional negligence, or regulatory breaches.

**8.3. Insurance:** The Partner must maintain valid professional indemnity insurance throughout this Agreement.

---

**9. TERM & TERMINATION**

**9.1. Term:** This Agreement remains valid until terminated.

**9.2. Termination for Convenience:** Either party may terminate with **30 days' written notice**.

**9.3. Immediate Termination for Cause:** The Platform may terminate this Agreement **IMMEDIATELY** if the Partner breaches privacy protocols, disparages the brand, or violates the Non-Circumvention clause.
