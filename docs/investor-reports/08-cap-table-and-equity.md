## 2. How to Protect Yourself (The 40%)

### Mechanism A: Vesting (The "Compliance" Frame)
**The Risk:** If you propose vesting as "protection," your co-founders might think you are planning to fire them.
**The Fix:** Frame it as an **Investor Requirement** (which it is).
*   **The Script:**
    > "I spoke to our advisors. No serious investor will write a check unless we *all* sign standard 4-year vesting agreements. It's a compliance box we have to check, just like registering at the Registrar General. If we don't have it, we are un-fundable."
*   **Result:** You get the protection, but the "Bad Guy" is the imaginary investor, not you.

### Mechanism B: Board Composition (Real Control)
**Critique:** "Super-Voting Shares" are rare/arrogant for a student pre-seed.
**The Better Move:** A **Board Composition Clause**.
*   **The Clause:** *"The Founders shall retain the right to appoint 2 out of 3 Board Directors so long as they collectively hold at least 20% of the company."*
*   **Why it works:** You don't need fancy stock classes. You just need the votes in the boardroom. If you + CTO are the 2 directors, you control the company.
